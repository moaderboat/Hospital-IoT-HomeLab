## CRITICAL SPECIFICATIONS

### DO NOT SUBSTITUTE:
- **Solenoid Valve**: MUST be "Normally Closed" (NC) - fail-safe
- **Raspberry Pi**: MUST be Pi 5 (different pinout than Pi 4)
- **HDMI Cable**: MUST be "Micro HDMI" (NOT Mini HDMI)
- **NPT Fittings**: MUST be NPT thread (NOT metric G1/4")
- **DataQ Sensors**: Analog 0.5-4.5V output, 1/8-27 NPT threaded (NOT I2C sensors like BMP388)
- **ADS1115**: 16-bit ADC required for analog sensors (NOT ADS1015 which is 12-bit)
- **BSS138 Level Shifter**: REQUIRED for 5V ADS1115 ↔ 3.3V Pi I2C (NOT optional)

### OK to Substitute Similar:
- Plywood (any furniture-grade birch 3/4" thick)
- Wood stain (any dark walnut)
- Foam boards (any 20×30" white)
