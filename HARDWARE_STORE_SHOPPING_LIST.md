## SHOPPING TIPS

### Before You Go:
✓ Call ahead to confirm plexiglass 24"×24" is in stock
✓ Ask if they can cut plywood to 24"×36" size (usually free)
✓ Verify Minwax product numbers (#2716 and #63333)

### At the Store:
✓ Ask lumber department for "furniture grade birch plywood"
✓ Inspect plywood for warping - lay flat and check edges
✓ For plexiglass, verify it's "cast acrylic" not "extruded" (cast is clearer)
✓ Get paint stirring sticks (free) for mixing stain

### Tools You'll Need (if you don't have):
- [ ] Foam brush or staining rag (for applying stain)
- [ ] 220-grit sandpaper (for smoothing before staining)
- [ ] Paint tray or disposable container (for polyurethane)
- [ ] Disposable gloves (for staining work)
